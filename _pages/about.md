¡Hola! I am a Peruvian PhD student in Comparative Politics at Cornell University. My research explores subnational politics, democracy, and women's political representation with a regional focus in Latin America.

Currently, I am a Graduate Fellow (2026–27) in the [Latin American and Caribbean Studies Program](https://einaudi.cornell.edu/discover/people/maria-elias-pineda) at the Mario Einaudi Center for International Studies and serve as an administrative coordinator at the [Red de Estudios sobre Política Subnacional en América Latina (REPSAL)](https://sites.google.com/view/redsubnacional/inicio).

Before joining Cornell in 2025, I worked as a research and teaching assistant at Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP) and as a researcher at Peru's electoral institutions (Oficina Nacional de Procesos Electorales and Registro Nacional de Identificación y Estado Civil). I earned a Bachelor's degree (with Licenciatura) in Political Science and Government from PUCP.

My research has been published in Latin American Research Review (forthcoming) and Revista Elecciones.
