Hi! I am a Peruvian PhD student in Comparative Politics at Cornell University, with a regional focus on Latin America. My research examines subnational politics, democracy, and women's political representation.

Currently, I am a Graduate Fellow (2026-27) in the Latin American and Caribbean Studies Program at the Mario Einaudi Center for International Studies and serve as an administrative coordinator at the Red de Estudios sobre Política Subnacional en América Latina (REPSAL).

Before joining Cornell, I worked as a research assistant at Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP) and as a researcher at Peru's electoral bodies (Oficina Nacional de Procesos Electorales and Registro Nacional de Identificación y Estado Civil). I earned a Bachelor's degree (with Licenciatura) in Political Science and Government from PUCP, graduating with the highest distinction.
